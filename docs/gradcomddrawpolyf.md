# **draw polyf**

`draw polyf `*`x1 y1 x2 y2 x3 y3 ... xn yn`*

Draw a filled polygon between a series of *`x,y`* points. The polygon is closed by having *`xn=x1`* and *`yn=y1`*. <a href="gradcomdsetline.html">`set line`</a> controls the fill color.
