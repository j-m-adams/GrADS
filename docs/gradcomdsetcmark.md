# **set cmark**

`set cmark `*`marktype`*

Set the style of the marker for line plots. *`marktype`* may be one of the following:

```text

 0 - none
 1 - plus sign
 2 - open circle (default)
 3 - closed circle 
 4 - open square 
 5 - closed square 
 6 - multiplication sign
 7 - open diamond 
 8 - open triangle 
 9 - closed triangle
10 - open circle with vertical bar
11 - closed circle with vertical bar

```

## Usage Notes

1.  Reset by `clear` or `display`.
2.  The size of the marker may be set by using <a href="gradcomdsetdigsize.html.html">`set digsize`</a>.
